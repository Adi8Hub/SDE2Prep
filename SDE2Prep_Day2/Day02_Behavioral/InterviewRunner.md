# Interview Runner – Day 2 Behavioral (Customer Obsession)

## 🎯 Focus: Customer Obsession
- Amazon's most critical leadership principle.
- Show how you prioritized customer satisfaction, even at cost or effort.

---

## 🧠 Prompt:
**"Tell me about a time you went above and beyond for a customer or user."**

---

## 📝 STAR Format Reminder:
- **S (Situation):** Set the context.
- **T (Task):** What was expected or the goal?
- **A (Action):** What exactly did *you* do?
- **R (Result):** Measurable outcome, preferably quantifiable.

---

## ✅ Checklist:
- [ ] Is the story relevant to customer or user interaction?
- [ ] Is the problem clearly explained (S/T)?
- [ ] Are your contributions distinct (A)?
- [ ] Is the result strong and data-backed (R)?
- [ ] Did you take initiative without being asked?
- [ ] Were long-term customer benefits prioritized?

---

## 🧠 Tip:
Use phrases like:
- “I noticed the user was struggling with…”
- “Though it wasn’t in the original scope, I…”
- “To improve retention/satisfaction, I proposed…”

---
